# Coffee Trend & Spend Analysis Dashboard (Interactive Dashboard using Microsoft Power BI)
## Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze coffee shop sales, customer spending patterns, product performance, and ordering trends across different days and time periods. The dashboard helps identify peak ordering hours, spending patterns, and customer behavior to support data-driven decisions related to staffing, inventory, promotions, and business planning.

## Dataset Used
- Coffee Shop Sales Dataset

## Questions (KPIs)
- What is the total spend?
- What is the total number of cups sold?
- What is the peak hour based on cups sold?
- Which hour has the highest number of tables?
- How does spending vary between weekdays and weekends?
- What is the average spend on weekdays and weekends?
- Which month has the highest spending?
- Which day of the week has the highest spend?
- Which time period has the highest spending?
- Which coffee products are ordered most frequently?
- How does customer spending change across different time periods?

## Process
- Collected and verified the coffee shop sales dataset.
- Cleaned and transformed the data using Power Query.
- Built relationships between order, product, and time-related data.
- Developed DAX measures for spend, cup sales, peak-hour analysis, and weekday/weekend comparisons.
- Created KPI cards for key business metrics.
- Designed interactive visualizations using trend lines, bar charts, donut charts, and product cards.
- Created multi-page navigation using Home, Order, Menu, Product, and Setting pages.
- Developed a visual product gallery to make the dashboard interactive and easy to explore.

## Project Insights
- Total Spend: **₹17K**
- Total Cups Sold: **486**
- Peak Hour Cups Sold: **51**
- Peak Hour Tables: **7**
- Weekday Spend: **₹12K**
- Weekend Spend: **₹4,981**
- Average Weekday Spend: **₹2,492**
- Average Weekend Spend: **₹2,491**

### Spending Patterns
- Spending peaked in **February (₹2.1K)** and **May (₹2.0K)**.
- Spending reached its lowest point around **November (₹0.9K)**.
- **Wednesday and Thursday** recorded the highest spend by day of week at **₹2.7K each**.
- **Night** was the leading time period for spending at **₹7.2K**, followed by **Afternoon (₹5.91K)** and **Morning (₹4.33K)**.
- Weekday spending was higher than weekend spending, while the average spend per period was almost the same.
